Gambit Realms: animated chess where every capture is a duel

Free chess game for beginners that runs in your browser. Pieces move and fight on every capture, in four themed realms: Enchanted Hall (wizard chess), Iron Keep (medieval), Neon Grid (cyberpunk) and Sakura Arena (anime).

Learn chess from scratch

Eight interactive lessons cover the board, every piece, check and checkmate, castling, en passant and how to keep your pieces safe. A coach warns you before a move that would lose material.

Eight measured opponents

Sleepy Pawn to Archmage, powered by Stockfish and rated on the Stockfish Elo scale. Every number is published on the Evidence page, including the targets that were missed.

Gambit Realms needs JavaScript. Please enable it and reload.
